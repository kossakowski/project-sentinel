# Clarified-policy v2 development-label review

## Scope and result

- This fixture contains the 34 preserved `development` cases from `model_comparison_first50.yaml` plus 2 additional synthetic v2 controls. It has 36 cases in 8 sequences and 5 synthetic cases in total.
- The annotations use only each preserved or synthetic article title/summary and the clarified policy. They do not use candidate-model outputs.
- The copy retains `version: 1` for the dataset envelope and root `policy_version: 2`.
- After the independent assistant review, the fixture contains 24 `reviewed` and 12 `disputed` labels. None is human-approved.
- The independent review is recorded in [model-comparison-v2-development-independent-review.md](model-comparison-v2-development-independent-review.md).
- No `policy_pending`, placeholder, or proposed-label field remains in the operative annotations.
- Every case has `expected.facts` with `attack_countries`, `protection`, and `status`, plus short direct-source excerpts in `provenance.evidence`. An empty excerpt records that the source contains no affirmative evidence for that field.

`affected_countries` means monitored countries with an explicitly reported local incident or protective response. It does not mean those countries were attacked. `facts.attack_countries` separately records the country of an explicit hostile physical impact/incursion, including Ukraine.

## Resolved operator choices and their application

- `near_border_strike = awareness`: a confirmed Ukraine-side strike just outside Poland is 5–6. None of the four preserved border snippets states which side of the shared border was struck, so all four remain disputed at 2–3 and silent. The independent synthetic sequence `synthetic-confirmed-ukraine-border-strike` positively tests the choice: its first explicit Ukraine-side report sends `initial` at 5–6, and its translated independent confirmation remains 5–6 but is `silent` as the same incident.
- `neutralised_drone = original_severity`: the retained annotation for the first report is 7–8 with one `initial` notification, but `mc-rusinowo-01` and `-02` are disputed because “Russian components/parts” plus detonation does not conclusively establish a Russian military drone. This notification chronology is provisional and conditional on independent review of those first reports.
- Later same-incident Rusinowo reports remain `silent`. The original-severity exception overrides the generic historical/reaction band only when the current source itself explicitly supports the recovered Russian military-drone classification; incident memory cannot lend origin or military nature.
- `facts.status` remains source-derived. Explicit neutralisation is `resolved`; mere past tense, a find, an airport closure, or a detention does not independently establish that danger ended. Unsupported status is `unclear` with no fabricated evidence quote.

There is no remaining prompt-policy contradiction in these annotations. Disputed initial events can prevent reference-mode incident creation, making later identity dimensions unscorable; notification labels are intentionally not rewritten around that evaluator consequence.

## Sequence-level notification chronology

- `border-train-near-poland-2026-09-13`: all four preserved records are 2–3 and silent because none confirms the strike’s side of the border. Their true same-episode relations are preserved; `mc-border-train-02` additionally has uncertain title-only identity evidence.
- `synthetic-confirmed-ukraine-border-strike`: `mc-synth-border-awareness-01` is the explicit Ukraine-side 5–6 `initial`; `mc-synth-border-awareness-02` is the same-incident independent translated confirmation at 5–6 and `silent`. No new wave is described.
- `poland-precautionary-scramble-2026-09-16`: `mc-scramble-pl-01` remains below 5 because “Alert” does not establish a resident order. `mc-scramble-pl-02` is the first concrete precaution and sends `initial`; `mc-scramble-pl-06` is the later explicit resident air-raid alert and sends `update` at 9–10. `mc-scramble-pl-05` is disputed because past-tense closure wording does not prove the precaution ended.
- `rusinowo-drone-recovery-2026-09-16`: the retained chronology has `mc-rusinowo-01` as the only 7–8 `initial` and every later report `silent`, but the first two military-nature labels are disputed. The chronology is conditional; in reference mode a disputed first event may not be materialized, and later identity can therefore be unscorable. Facts remain independently source-scored.
- `tryncza-civilian-drone-2026-09-16`: all three 1–4 silent labels are disputed because the airborne festival drone falls outside the explicit ground-find rule and detention does not prove resolution.
- `russian-assassination-network-2026-09-16`: the two uncertain cross-outlet identity labels remain disputed; the preserved `same_as` values are not promoted to approval.
